`runRelayrReviewed` runs ERC-2771 calls through Relayr under one review. Before the review it quotes stand-ins of the calls, signed by a key made in memory for that quote only, and shows the fee as "Up to X" (the quote plus `RELAYR_FEE_MARGIN_BPS`). When the calls need the real sender, a public call with the same chain, gas and calldata length is quoted instead; without any quote in time, the fee is bounded from a gas estimate through the forwarder as the sender (`RELAYR_FEE_MARKUP_BPS`). After confirm it signs the requests, checks each against the forwarder and simulates it, posts them for the quote that binds them, and pays without asking again when the fee is at most the cap; a higher fee opens one fresh review of the payment, and a quote that expires before the wallet opens is replaced the same way. The payment goes only to Relayr's pinned contract and selector, under its write hold (the action's `scope`, when given), and is proven from the chain. `signReviewedForwardRequests` and `payRelayrReviewed` are the single-purpose paths, each with its own review; every destination must trust the chain's canonical forwarder. `pollRelayrBundle` treats only `failed`, in any case, as final. `safeRelayrWalletDeps` supplies `review` and `sendPayment` for `createSafeRelayrController`.
