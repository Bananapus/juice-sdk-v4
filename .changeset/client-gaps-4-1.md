Gaps the four client landings on 4.0 found:

- `sendReviewed` and `resumeReviewed` report `done` only once the step's write hold verified its receipt. A receipt the hold has not verified yet (a lagging node, a same-nonce replacement, a block since reorged out) throws `ReviewedReceiptUnverifiedError` ("Confirming your transaction."); the step stays held, and in flight in its journal, until the hold settles it. `useReviewedRun`'s `run` resolves `{ status: "receipt", step, hash }` (`ReviewedRunReceipt`), never `failed`, for a step that was sent; its `phase` says `receipt` too, it follows the hold, and it ends `done` when the hold settles.
- A run is refused before its review while the action's last success is unacknowledged (`WriteHoldUnacknowledgedSuccessError`, "This went through while you were away.", with `success`), so a double click or a remount can't send it twice. `useReviewedRun` acknowledges a `done` it shows, and offers one it could not show as `sendAgainAction`; a direct caller acknowledges with the hold handle's `acknowledge(error.success)`.
- A payment the review-time simulation refused keeps the revert as its `cause` (the review's line, with the node's error and its revert data inside), so `decodePayFailure` reads it and the allowed-decrease retry works for that attempt.
- `viemWalletAdapter` checks the connection where the wallet is reached: every sending or signing provider request (`eth_sendTransaction`, `wallet_sendCalls`, `eth_signTypedData_v4`, `personal_sign` and the like) first re-reads the account, chain, Safe mode, the connector (new `connector`, wagmi's `connector?.uid`) and the site's own synchronous `check` (view-as, say), since viem awaits its own reads before it sends. A connector that changes while the wallet client loads is fetched again (three tries, then "Wallet connection changed"). Its refusals before any request went out, and a wallet client that can't be fetched, are `WalletNotInvokedError`: core counts them as unsent, releases the held step and frees a Relayr payment's quote (`relayrPaymentAttemptOutcome` is `unpaid`), and the site sees the refusal's own line, not a cancel. Only that class counts, never a field a provider sets or an error the wallet returned.
- `signReviewed` hands a self-reviewing wallet the raw typed data or text to review on its own page, with no in-app prompt (`onPreview` gets the model). `selfReviewingAdapter` takes `signTypedData` and `signMessage`; without them it still refuses with 4200.
- `nana-wallet-check` also catches `signaWalletAdapter`, `selfReviewingAdapter` and `registerReviewHandler` called through `.call`, `.apply` or `.bind`.
